Practitioners, researchers, AYUSH startups and cultivators navigate overlapping legal regimes with no authoritative guidance tool. The result: legitimate innovation is under-protected, while India's traditional knowledge remains exposed to misappropriation abroad.

🌿 Formulation Classifier

Know Your Product's IP Posture

IP-SAKTI asks the minimum clarifying questions to determine your product's regulatory category — each with a very different IP and ABS posture.

📜

Classical / Generic Medicine

Formulation and method drawn from a First-Schedule authoritative text (e.g., Charaka Samhita). Largely TK — faces §3(p) bar, defended through TKDL.

§3(p) Bar
🔬

Patent / Proprietary Medicine

New, undisclosed formula or manufacturing process. Novel combinations may be patentable if they cross the inventive-step threshold.

Patentable
🧪

New / Non-Classical Drug

Not found in authoritative texts; requires proof of safety and effectiveness via clinical evidence. Genuine patent potential with data exclusivity.

High IP Value
🌱

Phytopharmaceutical

Standardised plant-derived formulation meeting Schedule Y-A requirements. Sits between drug and nutraceutical; GI may apply.

GI Eligible
🥗

Ayurveda-Aahar / Nutraceutical

FSSAI-regulated health/dietary product. Lower drug burden; trademark and trade-dress protection key.

FSSAI
✨

Cosmetic

Governed by Drugs & Cosmetics Act Schedule S. Design and trademark protection central; efficacy claims regulated by ASCI.
